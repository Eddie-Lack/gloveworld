Glover Debug Patcher

Drop your Glover (USA) ROM to make the debug ROM. Controller 2 drives the team's debug controls, and the game's debug messages and input recordings go to the IS-Viewer log of your emulator or flashcart.

The ROM stays on your computer: the page patches it in the browser and sends nothing anywhere.

Patch

  1. Read the ROM and put it in .z64 byte order
    waiting for a file
  2. Check that it is Glover (USA)
    SHA-1 270be17b3c8da9b88a7b99c2a545b0bce16837f4
  3. Find the boot chip from the header checksum
  4. Check and replace the patched words
  5. Recompute the header checksum

Good to know

  • Plug in controller 2 before the console starts. Its stick, A and B move the helper box all the time; that is how the debug code works.
  • L can be saved into your save slot. Use a slot you don't mind.
  • To record, press Z once on the title screen with controller 2 and start a level. The intro level always plays back instead. Hold A and press B on controller 2 to save the recording.
  • Messages and recordings go to the IS-Viewer: ares and mupen64plus show them in their logs, and SummerCart64 sends them over USB. In mupen64plus they are the IS64: lines.
  • tools/patches/moolog.py turns the recording lines of a log into .moo files, and mooedit.html views and edits them.
